# random-entity (∀∃)

Regarding world models constructed by the human mind as alternative physics, I, **random-entity (∀∃)**, research and practice creative expressions that bridge the mathematical structures of alternative physics with psychological drama through computing technologies. I hope to stimulate the mental fluidity that awakens when we simultaneously recognize the machine within the nature and the nature within the machines.
